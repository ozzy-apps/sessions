# Sessions drum content

Native GM mappings of the six approved libraries. Audio is copied without modification. Original license notices and exact source provenance are in licenses/. CC0 banks: Karoryfer Big Rusty Drums, Swirly Drums and Frankensnare. CC BY 4.0 banks: DrumGizmo DRSKit and MuldjordKit, and Wilkinson Audio Naked Drums, including SFZ mappings by kinwie.

Sessions maps the main GM articulations and retains their recorded velocity layers and round robins. Close/alternate/overhead/room recordings are synchronized by velocity and sequence. Unmapped GM articulations fall back to the original recipe; Frankensnare is a snare expansion. Arbitrary SFZ scripts/effects, CC-driven articulations and all optional upstream kit-piece variations are not interpreted. Full upstream downloads remain in dist/drum-sources for additional sample use.

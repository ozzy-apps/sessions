# Osmium Wavefront factory bank

Sessions adds 48 authored sounds to Osmium, bringing the factory bank to 88. The original 40 Drum & Bass presets keep their names, parameter values, modulation routes and curves. Their appended oscillator controls remain at zero, which selects the original synthesis path.

Find the bank in Osmium's preset selector or search **Osmium** in Browser → Sounds. Every new patch uses the expanded wavetable catalog. Across the bank, all 40 added tables and all five added warp types are represented.

## Playing and macros

Pads and textures suit open chords around MIDI 52–72. Leads and keys suit MIDI 55–79. Bass patches suit MIDI 28–43; motion patches suit MIDI 43–67. These are starting ranges rather than fixed tuning requirements. Curves, LFOs and steppers follow the project tempo; none of the eight macros changes pitch or rhythmic division.

| Macro | Function |
|---|---|
| Tone | Opens both filters. |
| Warp | Increases oscillator 1's warp depth and scans oscillator 2's wavetable. |
| Drive | Adds filter drive and insert saturation. |
| Width | Widens unison voices; single-voice keys and subs add optional chorus. |
| Space | Adds reverb while preserving the dry signal. |
| Echo | Adds synced delay while preserving the dry attack. |
| Body | Adds the sub oscillator beneath oscillator 1. |
| Air | Adds a small amount of noise and high-shelf lift. |

The new spectral controls are used with restrained depths: Phase shapes the phase trajectory, Fold and the separate Fold control add harmonic density, Asym changes waveform balance, Steps supplies digital edges, and Cross FM uses the next oscillator as a phase-modulation source. Cross FM's source level can be silent in the audible mix; its table, position and pitch still determine the modulation. Several patches also route pressure, velocity, envelopes or drawn curves into spectral controls.

## Verification and reproducible audition

`tests/osmium_bank.cpp` checks normalized parameter values, unique and valid modulation routes, browser registration, complete set/preset round trips, all eight connected macros, finite output and headroom with all macros at maximum. The new patches additionally check dry release completion and compare their audible renders against other new patches in the same category. Keys and motion patches also render four-note chords at default settings and with every macro at maximum to check polyphonic headroom. The bank uses all added tables and warp types; the original bank has a captured content fingerprint.

Run the bank test with an output directory to export all 88 portable presets, a timestamped listening guide, a per-patch level report and one audition reel:

```sh
./build/sessions_osmium_bank dist/osmium-wavefront
```

The reel renders at 48 kHz / 174 BPM with a shared gain of 0.8 and short boundary fades. Patches are not individually peak-normalized. Pads and textures use open four-note chords; the other categories use short phrases. Files under `dist/` are local build artifacts and are not committed.

The final production-DSP audit passed **1,897 checks, zero failures**, including all 88 exported-preset round trips. The same checks without export perform 1,720 assertions. A separate captured render comparison kept the original 40 factory sounds and Monolith byte-identical after the oscillator extensions and phase-wrap fixes.

Its companion `Osmium Wavefront.md` gives relative timestamps, and `Wavefront Presets/` contains the new portable presets. New warp and fold shaping applies DC removal when active; the original synthesis path remains unchanged.

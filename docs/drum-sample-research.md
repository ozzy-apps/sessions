# Drum library candidates

Primary sources verified 19 October 2026. All six libraries were downloaded on 20 October and their main GM articulations are integrated in the optional Sessions 0.41 content package. The original AVL banks remain bundled. See the [integration guide](studio-expansion.md) for installation, mapped scope, recorded microphones and memory use. Specifications and automated playback checks are not an independent listening judgment of sound quality.

## Commercial use and redistribution

There are two separate uses to check: making music with samples, and shipping the samples inside an instrument. [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) permits copying, modification and commercial distribution without copyright attribution requirements. [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) permits commercial reuse and redistribution with appropriate credit, a license link and an indication of modifications. Keep upstream notices with a redistributed sample collection. This research records the licenses declared by the listed distributors, rather than making claims about unrelated downloads of the same kit.

## Implemented integration · 0.41

All six full sources are in ignored `dist/drum-sources/`. Tracked `content/drums/licenses/*/provenance.json` pins their release SHA-256 hashes or exact Git commits. The fetch script reproduces these revisions; preparation copies unmodified audio and original notices.

The native loader supports mono/stereo WAV and FLAC, velocity groups and aligned round robins across primary/alternate close, overhead and room recordings. Selected immutable banks are prepared outside callbacks. Global/per-piece library selectors append controls without changing old source meanings. [Counts, playback behavior and limitations](studio-expansion.md).

Further work can add more upstream kit variations, recorded mic streams, disk streaming and CC-driven articulations. Arbitrary SFZ scripts/effects are not interpreted. Physical microphone repositioning remains modeled.

Current AVL recordings already allow commercial music. Their separate [author license](https://www.bandshed.net/avldrumkits/) requires preserving attribution and the share-alike/renaming terms when redistributing a modified sample library; the author explicitly exempts produced music from share-alike and attribution requirements. Sessions' existing collection notices remain in `assets/drums/README.md`.
